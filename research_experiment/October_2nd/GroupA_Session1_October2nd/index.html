Task description:

We are playing a card memory game. Each picture is a card.

This game is divided into two sessions: (1) Learning and (2) Testing.
In this first session, the learning session, there will be several rounds.
In each round you will learn card associations in pairs.

Follow the instruction on each part of the game.

Try to remember the associations between the pair of pictures.

The pairs are presented in a random order, so you should only be learning the one pair at a time.
For example, in round 1 you may learn that a tennis ball card is paired with a penguin card and in round 2 you may learn that a strawberry card is paired with a jacket card. This does not mean that a penguin card is paired with a strawberry card.

A card can be paired to multiple cards.
For example, in round 3 you may learn that a tennis ball card is also paired with a scarf card.

To help you remember the associations, try to make a story connecting the 2 pictures.
For example, in a round if you learn that a strawberry card and a jacket card are a paired. You can remember this association by saying a sentence like, "In the morning I put on my jacket so that I wouldn't get cold while strawberry picking".

Note that though there will only be 24 different card pair associations that will be presented to you, these pairs will be presented repeatedly, making for a higher round number.
